      Help

      Tap a topic to open it.

      Getting started and working offline
      • Open the app once with a connection, then use your browser's menu: Add to Home screen or Install app.
      • Open it from the home screen icon from then on. It works with no connection.
      • Before going to site, check the top of the screen says Ready offline ✓. If not, open the app once more with a connection.
      • Everything stays on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
      Logging a session
      • Press START LOGGING at the same moment you start the sound level meter.
      • LOG MOMENT records the time the instant you tap it. Type a description before or after.
      • START RANGE then END RANGE marks something that lasts, like a truck idling.
      • Pause logging marks the paused time as excluded.
      • Press Stop session after you stop the meter. A stopped session can't be continued.
      Categories, descriptions and excluding
      • Tap one or more categories before logging. None tapped means General.
      • Descriptions are optional at the time: capturing when matters most. Fill them in later.
      • Tick Exclude from assessment for anything that shouldn't count, such as you talking or wind on the microphone. It is kept and shown, but left out of the assessed figures.
      • Tap any logged entry to change its description, categories or exclusion.
      Site and job details
      • Job, client, location, operator, meter, calibration, weather and noise environment.
      • They fill the Environmental Noise Measurement sheet on the laptop.
      • Fill them in before Start or any time after. All are optional.
      • Job, client, operator, meter and calibrator are remembered for the next position.
      Planned length (timer)
      • Enter the minutes before Start. The screen counts down.
      • When the time is up the tablet vibrates and the screen flashes. It makes no sound, so the meter isn't affected.
      • It doesn't stop anything itself: stop the meter, then press Stop session.
      Microphone and audio recording
      • What it's for: listening back to check what you heard, and lining observations up with the sounds on the laptop.
      • It is not a measurement. The tablet's microphone isn't calibrated; only the meter's data counts.
      • Untick it if you don't want a recording, for privacy or to save battery and storage. Logging works the same.
      • Allow the microphone when asked. The bar under the timer moves when sound is picked up.
      • Keep this screen open while logging. Switching apps can stop the recording on some tablets.
      • If the app closed by accident, reopen it: the session carries on. Tap Resume audio; the gap is filled with silence so the timing stays right.
      Listening back and reviewing
      • Tap ▶ on an entry to hear the few seconds before it (you log a sound after hearing it), or the start of a range. Tap again to stop.
      • While logging, use headphones. Through the speaker, the meter would hear the playback.
      • When you stop, the session opens for review: listen back and fill in notes before exporting.
      • Review in Saved sessions opens it again later.
      Photos
      • On an observation: tap it, then Add photo.
      • Of the measurement position: Site details, then Add photo (once the session has started).
      • Tap a photo to see it full size or delete it.
      • Photos are shrunk to save space and travel with the exported session.
      Exporting to the laptop and the field sheet
      • Tap Export on the session. On a tablet, Share sends it by email, cloud drive or Nearby Share; Save to Downloads keeps a copy on the device.
      • On the laptop, open the Acoustic Monitoring Software, choose Import and pick the .zip. The session, audio, details and photos arrive ready to bind to the meter's file.
      • The field sheet is produced on the laptop once the meter's file is bound, because its results come from the meter. What you entered here fills the rest of it.
      If something goes wrong
      • "Permission denied" on export or the microphone: try again first. The browser sometimes refuses the first attempt.
      • If it keeps failing, check this site's permissions in your browser's settings (Site settings or Privacy, or the padlock or site icon next to the address). Allow the microphone and don't block downloads.
      • If sharing is refused, the file is saved to Downloads instead.
      • Nothing is lost by retrying: sessions stay saved on the device until you delete them.
